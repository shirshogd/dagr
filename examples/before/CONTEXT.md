# Routing

| If the job is... | Go to |
|---|---|
| a weekly video script | `workflows/video-script/CONTEXT.md` |
| the weekly newsletter | `workflows/newsletter/CONTEXT.md` |

## Folders

- `knowledge/`: voice, audience, past topics (read-only reference)
- `inbox/`: Nora drops a one-line topic in `inbox/topic.md`
- `output/`: finished drafts, one folder per workflow
